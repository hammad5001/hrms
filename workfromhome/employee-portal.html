Message from Leadership

Leadership & Core
Message from the CEO

BALITECH was built on the belief that people, discipline and vision can create opportunities that change lives. Every organization grows through clients and revenue, but a lasting organization grows through standards. This manual defines those standards. It explains how we expect our people to work, lead, communicate, protect our clients, respect one another and represent the Company.

Our goal is not only to become a larger company. Our goal is to become a stronger, more organized and more trusted company. That requires clear policies, consistent leadership, fair enforcement and a culture where every person understands their responsibilities. As BALITECH continues to scale, this manual will help preserve what matters most: our people, our clients, our culture and our reputation.

Message from the General Manager

This manual has been written for practical use. It is not designed to sit in a file. It is meant to guide daily decisions on the floor, in HR, in operations, in training, in quality, in recruitment and in leadership. Every employee should know what is expected. Every manager should know what can be enforced. Every department should know how to operate with consistency.

BALITECH is performance-driven, people-first and innovation-led. These three ideas must work together. Performance without people creates pressure without loyalty. People-first without accountability creates comfort without growth. Innovation without discipline creates risk. Our culture is a blend of all three: high standards, real development and continuous improvement.

Non-Negotiable Policy Position

Attendance, compliance, confidentiality, workplace respect, data protection, ethical sales, professional conduct and client trust are non-negotiable at BALITECH. Policies are not suggestions; they are the operating standards of the Company.

Scope, Authority and Manual Use

Leadership & Core
Purpose: To establish a single reference point for employment standards, workplace policies and operating expectations.
Scope: All employees, leaders, interns, trainees, contractors, outsourced resources and authorized representatives.
Company Position

Scope, Authority and Manual Use is treated as a formal company policy area. The objective is not only to control behaviour, but to create a consistent operating standard across BALITECH. Every department is expected to implement this policy in a way that is fair, documented, practical and aligned with business needs. Where a campaign, client requirement, employment agreement, appointment letter, law or senior-management instruction provides a stricter standard, the stricter standard will apply. No employee or manager may use silence, custom, friendship, informal practice or past tolerance as permission to ignore this policy.

Non-Negotiable Standards
  • Policies must be applied consistently and fairly across departments.
  • Managers must explain relevant policies during onboarding, team huddles and coaching sessions.
  • Employees must read, understand and follow the latest approved version of the manual.
  • Specific appointment-letter terms, client rules or department SOPs must be read together with this manual.
Step-by-Step Procedure
1HR issues the latest approved manual.
2Employees sign the acknowledgement page.
3Managers review relevant rules with their teams.
4Department heads raise policy-change requests through HR and senior management.
Manager Responsibilities
  • Communicate the manual to new and existing employees.
  • Use the manual as the first reference during policy disputes.
  • Maintain evidence when a policy affects payroll, discipline, promotion or exit.
  • Escalate conflicts between manual, contract, law or client rule.
Employee Responsibilities
  • Read and follow the manual.
  • Ask HR or management if a rule is unclear.
  • Do not rely on informal practice against written policy.
  • Use the approved version, not old copies.
Examples & Practical Application
  • A verbal exception must be documented before it can be relied upon.
  • A department cannot create a rule that weakens a company-wide policy.
  • A stricter client compliance rule overrides a general manual rule.
Escalation & Consequences

Failure to follow the manual may lead to counseling, warning, disciplinary action, loss of benefits where permitted or termination depending on severity.

Implementation Note: This section should be read together with department SOPs, campaign rules and the employee's appointment letter. HR should maintain written records where this policy affects attendance, salary, incentive, discipline, promotion, transfer, final settlement or termination.

Employment Principles and Equal Opportunity

Hiring & Employment
Purpose: To define fair employment standards based on merit, performance, conduct and business need.
Scope: All employees, applicants, hiring managers and departments.
Company Position

Employment Principles and Equal Opportunity is treated as a formal company policy area. The objective is not only to control behaviour, but to create a consistent operating standard across BALITECH. Every department is expected to implement this policy in a way that is fair, documented, practical and aligned with business needs. Where a campaign, client requirement, employment agreement, appointment letter, law or senior-management instruction provides a stricter standard, the stricter standard will apply.

Non-Negotiable Standards
  • Employment decisions must be based on capability, merit, performance, behaviour and business need.
  • Harassment, discrimination, favoritism and abuse of authority are not tolerated.
  • Employees must provide truthful information during hiring and employment.
  • No person may promise permanent employment, salary revision or promotion without written approval.
Step-by-Step Procedure
1Define job requirements before hiring.
2Assess candidates fairly.
3Maintain employee files.
4Document role changes, transfers and employment decisions.
Manager Responsibilities
  • Apply employment decisions consistently.
  • Avoid personal bias in hiring or promotion.
  • Escalate potential conflicts of interest.
  • Protect candidate and employee information.
Employee Responsibilities
  • Provide accurate documents and personal information.
  • Follow reporting lines and assigned duties.
  • Cooperate with lawful and reasonable instructions.
  • Report unfair treatment or harassment concerns.
Examples & Practical Application
  • A strong referral still requires proper interview and selection.
  • False educational or employment documents may lead to termination.
  • A manager cannot promise salary beyond approved terms.
Escalation & Consequences

False information, discriminatory conduct or manipulation of employment records may lead to disciplinary action or termination.

Implementation Note: HR maintains written records where this policy affects attendance, salary, incentive, discipline, promotion, transfer, final settlement or termination.

Recruitment, Selection and Joining

Hiring & Employment
Purpose: To ensure professional, documented and controlled hiring and onboarding.
Scope: Recruitment, HR, department heads, hiring managers, trainers and all candidates.
Company Position

Recruitment, Selection and Joining is treated as a formal company policy area. The objective is to create a consistent hiring operating standard across BALITECH.

Non-Negotiable Standards
  • Hiring must begin through an approved headcount or replacement request.
  • Candidates must be evaluated for skills, communication, attitude, integrity and role fit.
  • New hires must complete HR onboarding before receiving system access.
  • Production employees must receive campaign, compliance and confidentiality orientation before live work.
Step-by-Step Procedure
1Submit hiring request.
2Screen and shortlist candidates.
3Conduct interviews, assessments and voice/process checks.
4Issue appointment documents and complete joining checklist.
Manager Responsibilities
  • Define role expectations and salary range before interviews.
  • Avoid unauthorized promises.
  • Ensure selected candidates are trained before live production.
  • Confirm system access only after HR authorization.
Employee Responsibilities
  • Submit required documents honestly and on time.
  • Attend orientation and training.
  • Sign required policy acknowledgements.
  • Clarify reporting line, shift and expectations.
Examples & Practical Application
  • A candidate cannot be allowed to use another employee's login.
  • A manager must not bypass HR checks for a friend or relative.
  • Skipping compliance training to fill a seat creates risk.
Escalation & Consequences

Recruitment manipulation, fake interviews, unauthorized offers or missing onboarding may result in disciplinary action against responsible personnel.

Implementation Note: Read together with department SOPs, campaign rules and appointment letters.

Probation, Confirmation and Employment Status

Hiring & Employment
Purpose: To assess suitability, performance, conduct and cultural fit before confirmation.
Scope: All probationary, permanent, trainee, intern, temporary and management employees.
Company Position

Probation, Confirmation and Employment Status is treated as a formal company policy area. The objective is consistent operating standards across BALITECH.

Non-Negotiable Standards
  • Probation is a formal assessment period, not a guaranteed confirmation.
  • Confirmation must be documented by HR or authorized management.
  • Managers must provide feedback during probation, not only at the end.
  • Employment status, transfer or role change must be documented.
Step-by-Step Procedure
1Track probation dates.
2Conduct midpoint and final reviews.
3Issue confirmation, extension or separation decision.
4Update status and reporting line in HR records.
Manager Responsibilities
  • Coach probationary employees early.
  • Document performance and attendance issues.
  • Recommend confirmation or extension with reasons.
  • Avoid promising confirmation without approval.
Employee Responsibilities
  • Meet performance, attendance and conduct standards.
  • Ask for feedback when expectations are unclear.
  • Complete training and assessments.
  • Follow the same policies as confirmed employees.
Examples & Practical Application
  • Strong sales but poor discipline may still delay confirmation.
  • Good attendance but poor skill readiness may require retraining.
  • Role transfer does not remove confidentiality obligations.
Escalation & Consequences

Failure to meet probation standards may result in extension, reassignment, non-confirmation or termination according to policy and law.

Implementation Note: Maintain written records for confirmation, extension, or separation.

Departments, Roles and Reporting Lines

Hiring & Employment
Purpose: To clarify reporting structure, department responsibilities and escalation channels.
Scope: All departments including Operations, People & Culture, Talent Acquisition, Training, QA, Compliance, IT/Dialer, Finance, Admin and Social Media.
Company Position

Departments, Roles and Reporting Lines ensures clear accountability and reporting channels.

Non-Negotiable Standards
  • Every employee must know their reporting manager and department.
  • Routine instructions should follow the chain of command.
  • Serious concerns may be escalated directly to HR or senior management.
  • Cross-department work must not create confusion over authority.
Step-by-Step Procedure
1Maintain organization chart.
2Issue role descriptions.
3Update reporting lines on transfer or promotion.
4Review responsibilities every six to twelve months.
Manager Responsibilities
  • Communicate reporting changes quickly.
  • Prevent employees from receiving conflicting instructions.
  • Escalate issues that affect salary, discipline, client commitments or system access.
  • Keep role boundaries clear.
Employee Responsibilities
  • Follow assigned reporting line.
  • Do not bypass managers for routine matters.
  • Escalate serious issues through appropriate channels.
  • Understand department-specific SOPs.
Examples & Practical Application
  • A support department cannot promise operational exceptions without Operations approval.
  • A manager cannot permanently move an employee without HR/Operations record.
  • Harassment or data-leak concerns can be escalated directly.
Escalation & Consequences

Unauthorized reporting-line changes or misuse of authority may lead to corrective action.

Implementation Note: Keep reporting lines up-to-date in HRMS records.

Working Hours, Shifts and Scheduling

Attendance & Leaves
Purpose: To maintain operational coverage, client delivery and employee discipline.
Scope: All day shift, night shift, rotational, campaign-based, support and management employees.
Company Position

Working Hours, Shifts and Scheduling maintains operational coverage and employee discipline across all shifts.

Non-Negotiable Standards
  • Working hours may vary by campaign, department, client requirement, season or business need.
  • Employees must be ready at their workstation before shift start.
  • Breaks must be taken responsibly and must not damage service levels.
  • Schedule changes require authorized approval.
Step-by-Step Procedure
1Publish or communicate schedules.
2Confirm weekly offs and special coverage.
3Record attendance through approved system.
4Approve schedule changes before implementation.
Manager Responsibilities
  • Plan staffing according to volume and targets.
  • Communicate shift changes clearly.
  • Monitor breaks and coverage.
  • Maintain evidence for overtime or extra-hour approvals.
Employee Responsibilities
  • Report on time and follow assigned schedule.
  • Request shift changes in advance.
  • Do not leave workstation without approval.
  • Return from breaks on time.
Examples & Practical Application
  • Swapping shifts with another employee requires manager approval.
  • Leaving early because volume is low is not allowed without approval.
  • Coverage during peak campaigns may require adjusted schedules.
Escalation & Consequences

Unauthorized absence from scheduled duty may lead to deductions, warnings, loss of incentives or disciplinary action.

Implementation Note: Shift changes and overtime records are logged in HRMS.

Attendance, Late Arrival and Punctuality

Attendance & Leaves
Purpose: To establish attendance discipline and clear late-arrival expectations.
Scope: All employees and leaders working onsite, remote or hybrid.
Company Position

Attendance, Late Arrival and Punctuality is a basic professional obligation essential for operational delivery.

Non-Negotiable Standards
  • Attendance is a basic professional obligation.
  • Employees may be required to report up to fifteen minutes before shift for login, huddle or setup.
  • Grace periods, if any, are not a right and may be withdrawn.
  • Repeated lateness affects confirmation, incentives, promotion and performance ratings.
Step-by-Step Procedure
1Mark attendance through approved system (biometric/portal punch).
2Verify team presence at shift start.
3Record late arrival and early departure.
4Escalate repeated patterns to HR.
Manager Responsibilities
  • Lead by example in punctuality.
  • Review daily attendance.
  • Address late patterns immediately.
  • Do not hide late arrivals or missed punches.
Employee Responsibilities
  • Plan travel and arrival responsibly.
  • Report genuine delays promptly.
  • Correct missed punch the same day if system issue occurred.
  • Be ready to work at shift start, not only present in building.
Examples & Practical Application
  • Arriving at the gate at shift start but reaching workstation late may count as late.
  • A manager arriving late weakens discipline enforcement.
  • Repeated short delays may form a serious pattern.
Escalation & Consequences

Late arrival may result in counseling, written warning, salary deduction where permissible, loss of punctuality bonus, final warning or termination for repeated misconduct.

Implementation Note: Attendance and late logs are integrated with monthly payroll cutoff.

No-Call/No-Show, Absconding and Sandwich Dock

Attendance & Leaves
Purpose: To control unauthorized absence and misuse of holidays/weekly offs.
Scope: All employees, leaders and departments.
Company Position

Unauthorized absence severely impacts service levels and is strictly governed by progressive action and sandwich dock rules.

Non-Negotiable Standards
  • Failure to report for duty without notice is a serious violation.
  • Managers must attempt reasonable contact and document the case.
  • Repeated no-call/no-show may be treated as job abandonment.
  • Unauthorized absence before or after weekly off/holiday may trigger sandwich dock subject to policy and law.
Step-by-Step Procedure
1Mark absence and attempt contact.
2Send notice where appropriate.
3Review employee explanation and evidence.
4Apply sandwich dock or abandonment process through HR approval.
Manager Responsibilities
  • Document contact attempts.
  • Inform HR promptly.
  • Secure Company assets and system access if risk exists.
  • Apply rules consistently, not selectively.
Employee Responsibilities
  • Inform manager before absence whenever possible.
  • Use official channels, not only friends or colleagues.
  • Submit emergency evidence where requested.
  • Return on approved date.
Examples & Practical Application
  • Sleeping through shift and informing after shift may still be no-call/no-show.
  • Absent before Eid holidays without approval may trigger sandwich policy.
  • Consecutive unexplained absence may be abandonment.
Escalation & Consequences

Consequences may include unpaid absence, loss of bonus, loss of unvested incentives, warning, suspension, termination or abandonment processing.

Implementation Note: HR reviews and authorizes sandwich dock deductions during payroll calculations.

Leave Policy

Attendance & Leaves
Purpose: To define paid, unpaid, casual, sick, emergency and special leave procedures.
Scope: All employees and departments.
Company Position

Leave Policy provides fair rest and emergency support while safeguarding operational continuity.

Non-Negotiable Standards
  • Leave requires approval; informing someone is not the same as approval.
  • Business continuity must be protected before approving leave.
  • Statutory minimums must not be reduced where applicable.
  • Leave during peak campaigns may be restricted or deferred.
Step-by-Step Procedure
1Submit leave request through the HRMS Request Hub / Leaves tab.
2Manager reviews team coverage.
3HR checks balance and eligibility.
4Approval or rejection is communicated in writing or approved system.
Manager Responsibilities
  • Review staffing before recommending leave.
  • Avoid favoritism in approvals.
  • Escalate suspicious patterns.
  • Send approved status to HR/payroll.
Employee Responsibilities
  • Apply in advance where possible.
  • Provide truthful reason and evidence if requested.
  • Return on approved date.
  • Do not take rejected leave.
Examples & Practical Application
  • Medical evidence may be required for repeated sick leave.
  • Leave around weekends/holidays may be reviewed for pattern.
  • Emergency leave must be communicated as soon as possible.
Escalation & Consequences

Unauthorized leave may be treated as unpaid absence, no-call/no-show, misconduct or abandonment depending on facts.

Implementation Note: Leave balances and half-day credits are tracked digitally in HRMS.

Payroll

Compensation & Benefits
Purpose: To manage salary, deductions, benefits and employee records accurately.
Scope: All employees, HR, Finance, Payroll and department heads.
Company Position

Payroll ensures timely, accurate, transparent and confidential salary disbursement.

Non-Negotiable Standards
  • Salary processing must be based on approved attendance and employment records.
  • Payroll changes require written authorization.
  • Salary information is strictly confidential.
  • Benefits depend on eligibility, service period, policy and provider rules.
Step-by-Step Procedure
1Lock attendance and payroll data on cutoff date.
2Verify deductions, bonuses and benefits.
3Process salaries according to approved records.
4Resolve queries through HR/Finance window.
Manager Responsibilities
  • Submit attendance, reports and incentive data on time.
  • Avoid unauthorized salary promises.
  • Keep salary information confidential.
  • Escalate payroll errors with evidence.
Employee Responsibilities
  • Verify bank details and payroll information.
  • Raise payroll queries promptly within the query window.
  • Submit accurate dependent/insurance information.
Examples & Practical Application
  • A missed punch not corrected before cutoff may delay adjustment to the following cycle.
Escalation & Consequences

Payroll manipulation, fake attendance or false benefit claims may lead to recovery, discipline or termination.

Implementation Note: HR and Finance maintain digital audit trails for all disbursements.

Annual Increment and Salary Revision

Compensation & Benefits
Purpose: To define yearly salary growth and off-cycle salary revisions.
Scope: All eligible employees unless a different written agreement applies.
Company Position

Annual increment recognizes continuous service, performance and commitment.

Non-Negotiable Standards
  • After one year of continuous employment, employees become eligible for a 10% annual increment subject to performance and approval.
  • Increment is not automatic where there is poor performance, misconduct or attendance issues.
  • Salary revisions outside the annual cycle require written approval.
  • No manager may promise salary changes without authority.
Step-by-Step Procedure
1Identify employees completing one year of tenure.
2Review KPI, attendance and disciplinary record.
3Manager submits recommendation to HR/Finance.
4HR, Finance and senior management approve and issue formal letter.
Manager Responsibilities
  • Recommend based on documented performance.
  • Do not promise increment before formal approval.
  • Consider compliance, attendance and conduct together.
  • Communicate decisions professionally.
Employee Responsibilities
  • Maintain performance and attendance standards.
  • Understand that increment depends on review and approval.
  • Do not rely on verbal promises.
  • Sign increment letters when issued.
Examples & Practical Application
  • A high seller with compliance violations may have increment deferred.
  • A consistent employee with strong discipline may qualify for full increment.
  • Promotion increase may affect annual increment cycle.
Escalation & Consequences

Manipulating reviews or making unauthorized salary promises may lead to disciplinary action.

Implementation Note: Increment letters are issued and archived in the employee digital file.

Incentives, Commissions and Performance Bonuses

Compensation & Benefits
Purpose: To define how performance-based earnings are earned, validated and paid.
Scope: Employees eligible for incentives, commissions, spiffs or bonuses.
Company Position

Incentives and bonuses reward genuine, validated, compliant results.

Non-Negotiable Standards
  • Incentives are payable only after Company/client validation.
  • QA, compliance, chargebacks and attendance may affect payout.
  • Fraudulent or non-compliant performance cannot be rewarded.
  • Campaign-specific incentive rules override assumptions.
Step-by-Step Procedure
1Publish incentive plan.
2Track and validate performance.
3Review QA/compliance status.
4Approve payout sheet and process payment.
Manager Responsibilities
  • Submit accurate production data.
  • Explain incentive rules clearly.
  • Escalate disputes within timeframe.
  • Do not manipulate numbers or dispositions.
Employee Responsibilities
  • Understand campaign payout rules.
  • Maintain compliance and attendance eligibility.
  • Raise disputes with evidence.
  • Do not assume unvalidated performance is payable.
Examples & Practical Application
